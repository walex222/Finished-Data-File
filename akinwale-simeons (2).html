How an engagement runs

Four stages, always in this order. Nothing gets designed before it's been diagnosed.

01

Diagnose

We spend time inside the business — data, conversations, operations — to find the real constraint, not the assumed one.

02

Design

We build a specific plan against that constraint, with clear priorities, owners, and a realistic timeline.

03

Deploy

We stay embedded through implementation — this is where most plans quietly fail, so we don't leave at the handoff.

04

Deliver

We review outcomes against the original goals and hand over a plan your team can run without us.

Common questions

How long does a typical engagement run?

It depends on scope. A focused diagnostic can take a few weeks; a full strategy-through-implementation engagement typically runs three to six months.

What size of business do you work with?

We work with growing, founder-led businesses through to established mid-size companies. What matters most is that leadership is ready to act on what we find.

Do you work with businesses remotely?

Yes — engagements are run in person where useful and remotely otherwise. Get in touch and we'll confirm fit for your location.

How is an engagement priced?

Fixed fee per phase, agreed upfront after the initial consultation, based on scope — not hourly billing.

What happens in the first consultation?

A 30-minute call to understand your business and where things stand. We'll tell you honestly whether we think we can help.
